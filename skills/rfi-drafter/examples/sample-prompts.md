# Sample prompts

Prompts that should trigger this skill. Attach or paste the relevant sheets
and spec sections in each case.

- "Draft an RFI: the duct chase on M-401 lands on the beam at grid C/4 on S-201. Sheets attached."
- "Write an RFI to the architect. The door schedule on A-601 says door 214 is 3'-0" but the plan on A-201 scales at 3'-6". Next number is RFI-112."
- "Spec 03 30 00 calls for 4,000 psi concrete at slabs on grade but the structural general notes on S-001 say 3,500. Which governs? Put it in RFI form."
- "The finish schedule doesn't call out a floor finish for room 118. Write the RFI and the log entry; we need it before the flooring sub mobilizes on the 20th."
- "Turn these three coordination issues from today's BIM meeting into RFIs. Screenshots and sheet numbers are in the attached notes."
- "How should I word an RFI asking whether the footing at grid 7 can be stepped? Here's S-101 and the geotech excerpt."
- "Review this RFI I drafted and tighten the question and references before I send it."

Prompts that should not trigger this skill:

- "Draft a submittal for the roofing membrane." (submittals are product data and shop drawings for review, not questions)
- "Write a change order request for the added duct offset." (pricing and entitlement, not an RFI)
- "Send a delay notice to the owner about the late RFI response." (contract notice; check the contract and the PM, not this skill)
- "Summarize all the open RFIs in the log." (log reporting, not drafting)
- "What is the standard RFI turnaround under AIA A201?" (contract interpretation question; answer directly with the caveat that it is not legal advice)
