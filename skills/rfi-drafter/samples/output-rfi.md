# RFI-047: Grid C/4, Level 2: duct riser DR-2 conflicts with W24x76 beam

**Conflict confirmed.** Section 2/M-401 places the DR-2 riser chase as a
3'-4" x 2'-0" floor opening centered on grid C at grid 4. S-201 shows a
W24x76 continuous along grid C from grid 3 to grid 5 at that location, with
no floor opening shown, and S-201 General Note 7 prohibits openings through
beams without the Structural Engineer's written approval. The two sheets
cannot both be built as drawn.

## Question

Please confirm whether duct riser DR-2 may be relocated north of grid C at
grid 4 so that its 3'-4" x 2'-0" floor opening sits in the composite deck
between the W16x26 infill beams, clear of the W24x76, with the opening
framed per S-201 General Note 8, or provide an alternate location or
framing for the riser.

Section 2/M-401 shows DR-2 rising through the Level 2 floor centered on
grid C at grid 4. S-201 shows the W24x76 continuous along grid C at that
location and no floor opening. As drawn, the riser opening falls on the
beam.

## Suggested resolution

For design-team confirmation, propose relocating the DR-2 chase north of grid C at grid 4 so the 3'-4" x 2'-0"
opening lands in the composite deck between adjacent W16x26 infill beams
(10'-0" o.c. per S-201), clear of the W24x76, and frame the opening with
headers per S-201 Note 8 (detail 4/S-501 to be confirmed by the Structural
Engineer). The riser offset from the
Level 1 mechanical room would be made below the Level 2 floor within the
30-degree limit of M-401 Note 6, and the 36" x 20" duct size would be
unchanged, consistent with 23 31 13 paragraph 3.3.C. Offered for the design
team's confirmation; this option would move the riser while retaining the
beam. The available offset height and clearances still need verification.

## Impact

- Cost: Potential. Relocating the chase may affect the shaft walls at grid
  C/4 on Levels 1 and 2 (architectural sheets not yet reviewed) and adds
  header framing at the new opening. Nothing has been priced.
- Schedule: Potential. Steel shop drawings are with the fabricator and the
  fabrication release is September 25, 2026; the fabricator needs one week
  to incorporate a framed opening. Duct shop drawings are due in three
  weeks and cannot be completed until the riser location is confirmed.
- The requested response date (September 18) is 10 calendar days from
  issue, shorter than the 10-working-day contract turnaround (September 22),
  assuming Monday-Friday working days with no holidays, because of the fabrication release. If the issue becomes a change, the
  project manager should check the contract's notice provisions; this RFI is
  not a notice and not legal advice.
