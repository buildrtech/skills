# Sample prompts

Prompts that should trigger this skill. Attach the bids in each case, and the
scope sheet or spec sections for the package when you have them.

- "Level these three drywall bids against our 09A scope sheet and tell me where the gaps are."
- "Build a bid tab for the electrical package. Four quotes attached, two are emails."
- "Compare these subcontractor quotes for site concrete. Who's really low once we plug the excluded scope?"
- "Here are the mechanical bids and my plug decisions (attached JSON). Rerun the leveled comparison."
- "Northgate excludes firestopping and Summit excludes insulation. Put them on the same basis and show me the plug summary."
- "Give me the leveled comparison as an Excel workbook so I can hand it to the PM."
- "Which of these roofing bids are missing the tapered insulation, and what questions should I send each bidder?"

- "Compare two separately identified options from the same company with the other bid; preserve each option and its alternate labels."

Prompts that should not trigger this skill:

- "Estimate this drywall package from scratch." (a takeoff and pricing pass with the user's own cost data, not leveling)
- "Write the subcontract for the low bidder." (contracting happens after the award decision)
- "Pick the winner for me." (the skill shows the leveled totals and their assumptions; the award decision is the team's)
- "Pull a firestopping unit price from a cost database to fill the gap." (plugs come from the estimator with a source, never from general data)
- "Should we bid this project?" (bid/no-bid intake, a different skill)
