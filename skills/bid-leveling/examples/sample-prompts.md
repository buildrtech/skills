# Sample prompts

## Should trigger

- "Bids for 09A closed at 2:00. Level them against the scope sheet and show me who's really low."
- "Here are three electrical quotes: two PDFs and one in the email below. Build me a bid tab spreadsheet."
- "Northgate excludes sheathing and Ridgeline excludes firestopping. Put them on the same basis."
- "Which of these roofing bids are missing the tapered insulation, and what should I ask each bidder?"
- "Ridgeline sent a revised number this morning. Use the revision and rebuild the workbook."
- "Use $9,800 from our firestopping budget as Northgate's plug. Who's low now?"

## Should not trigger

- "Estimate this drywall package from scratch." (takeoff and pricing, not leveling)
- "Roll all 30 packages up into the GMP." (whole-project roll-up)
- "Write the subcontract for the low bidder." (contracting, after the award)
- "Pick the winner for me." (the workbook shows the leveled totals and their assumptions; the award is the team's call)
- "Should we bid this project?" (bid/no-bid intake)
