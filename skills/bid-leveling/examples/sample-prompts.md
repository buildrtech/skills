# Sample prompts

## Should trigger

- "Bids for 09A closed at 2:00. Level them against the scope sheet and show me who's really low."
- "Build a bid tab for the electrical package. Four quotes attached, two are just emails."
- "Northgate excludes firestopping and Summit excludes insulation. Put them on the same basis."
- "Which of these roofing bids are missing the tapered insulation, and what should I ask each bidder?"
- "Ridgeline sent a revised number this morning. Use the revision and rerun the tab."
- "Carry the bond in every bid and accept Alternate 1. Who's low now?"
- "Here's the scenario I exported yesterday. Rebuild the tab with Summit's answers."
- "Give me the bid tab in Excel so I can hand it to the PM."

## Should not trigger

- "Estimate this drywall package from scratch." (takeoff and pricing, not leveling)
- "Roll all 30 packages up into the GMP." (whole-project roll-up)
- "Write the subcontract for the low bidder." (contracting, after the award)
- "Pick the winner for me." (the tab shows the leveled totals and their assumptions; the award is the team's call)
- "Should we bid this project?" (bid/no-bid intake)
