## Bid 1: Northgate Drywall & Acoustics (northgate-proposal.pdf, 2 pages)

**Page 1, Scope of Work.** Furnish and install non-structural metal framing,
gypsum board, shaft wall at elevator and mechanical shafts, taped and finished

blocking for casework and wall-mounted equipment per A-501 details.

Daily cleanup of our debris to GC-provided dumpster included. Pricing assumes
one mobilization per floor and clear access to each floor by hoist or stair.

**Page 2, Exclusions.** Exterior sheathing by others. Firestopping NIC.
Acoustical ceilings NIC.

**Page 2, Alternates.** Alternate No. 1, Level 5 finish at all corridors:
ADD $18,400.00.

**Page 2, Unit Prices.** Additional soffit/bulkhead, framed and finished:
$42.00 per LF.

**Page 2, Terms.** Base bid $842,500.00. Sales tax included. Performance and
payment bond if required add 1.5%. Proposal valid 30 days from August 21, 2026.
