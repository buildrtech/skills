## Scope sheet: 09A Metal framing and gypsum board (`09A-scope-sheet.md`)

**Project:** Cedar Hollow Family Health Clinic, two-story, 24,000 SF, Cedar
Hollow, MN
**Sections:** 09 22 16 Non-Structural Metal Framing; 09 21 16 Gypsum Board
Assemblies
**Bids due:** August 21, 2026, 2:00 p.m.

**Addenda**

- Addendum 1 (August 3): revised partition schedule on A-601. No change to
  09A scope.
- Addendum 2 (August 10): adds Level 5 finish at the two-story lobby. Requests
  Alternate 1, Level 5 finish at all corridors.

**09A includes**

1. Non-structural metal stud framing
2. Gypsum board, tape and finish to Level 4
3. Level 5 finish at the two-story lobby (Addendum 2)
4. Shaft wall at elevator and mechanical shafts
5. Sound attenuation batts in partitions noted on A-601
6. In-wall blocking for casework and wall-mounted equipment
7. Lifts and scaffolding for this trade's work
8. Daily cleanup to the GC's dumpster
9. Exterior glass-mat gypsum sheathing
10. Firestopping at rated penetrations made by this trade

**Not in 09A:** Acoustical ceilings (09 51 13) are bid separately as package
09B.

**Alternate 1 (add):** Level 5 finish at all corridors.

**Bond and tax:** State whether the price includes a performance and payment
bond and sales tax on materials. If a bond is not included, state the rate.
