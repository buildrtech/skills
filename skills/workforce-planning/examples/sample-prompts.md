# Sample prompts

## Should trigger
- Use these roster, PTO, assignments, and open-demand CSVs to plan next week's crews. Show who is overloaded and what demand remains after your proposed moves.
- Our foremen are allocated as percentages of different workweeks. Reconcile capacity using the attached calendars and identify conditional coverage options.
- Compare two staffing proposals without changing the scheduling system.

## Should not trigger
- Calculate payroll taxes or determine prevailing wages.
- Rank employees by performance or decide who to lay off.
- Forecast project revenue from contract values.
- Draft a closeout checklist.
