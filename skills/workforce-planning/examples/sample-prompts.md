# Sample prompts

## Should trigger

- "Get Thursday's staffing meeting ready. Staffing sheet, project list, and CRM export attached."
- "Who's overloaded over the next six months, and which jobs still have empty seats?"
- "If we win Riverside, who runs it? We'd need an ICRA-qualified super by March."
- "Tony retires end of March and Arvada runs through May. Who can take it?"
- "Union Station slipped to July. What does that do to the rest of the plan?"
- "Do we need to hire a superintendent this year? Weight the pipeline by win probability."
- "Here's last month's scenario export. Rerun it with the new staffing sheet."

## Should not trigger

- "Schedule the framing crew for next week." (craft crew scheduling)
- "Calculate overtime and payroll for the drywall crew." (payroll)
- "Rank our superintendents by performance." (performance review)
- "Which jobs should we bid?" (bid/no-bid intake)
