01 / Summary

Executive summary

Our team proposes a coordinated preconstruction and construction approach that keeps clinical operations protected while moving the renovation through a controlled 16-week route.

16 weeks

Planned construction duration with phased turnover.

$1.25M

Conceptual construction budget before owner alternates.

02 / Workstreams

Preconstruction control

Existing-condition review, buyout, infection-control planning, and long-lead procurement start before mobilization.

Weeks 1–3

North wing delivery

Selective demolition, rough-in, inspections, and above-ceiling close are sequenced away from active clinical routes.

Weeks 4–9

South wing turnover

Finishes, equipment coordination, commissioning support, and patient-ready turnover are bundled into a single closeout lane.

Weeks 10–16

03 / Priorities

Maintain safe access, reduce change-order exposure, and present clear weekly cost and schedule decisions.
