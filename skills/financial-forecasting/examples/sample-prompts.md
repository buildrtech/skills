# Sample prompts

Prompts that should trigger this skill. Each assumes the Buildr MCP server
is connected.

- "Give me the Q1 revenue forecast for work in progress."
- "What does our profit forecast look like by quarter for WIP and pursuits through the end of next year?"
- "How is the Harbor Street parking structure tracking? Percent complete, margin, and are we over or under billed?"
- "Which projects moved margin more than two points since the last close?"
- "Why did margin drop on Cedar Ridge between December and January?"
- "Close February on Northgate. Cost to date is $16.2M, billed $17.1M, EAC unchanged."
- "We need to push the start date on Riverside Clinic back a month. Can we?"
- "Show me the over/under billing position across everything in construction as of the last close."
- "Break down the forecast for backlog by market sector for next year."
- "What's the probability-weighted pipeline for pursuits in Q3?"

Prompts that should not trigger this skill:

- "Build the budget for this estimate." (estimating, not forecasting)
- "Prepare our WIP schedule for the bank." (this skill provides the inputs; the accountant prepares the schedule)
- "Which pursuits should we chase next quarter?" (business development; use the forecast only as one input)
