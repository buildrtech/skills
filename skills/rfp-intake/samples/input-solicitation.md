## Invitation to Bid CHPS-2026-07 (`CHPS-2026-07-invitation-to-bid.pdf`)

**Page 1**

### INVITATION TO BID

Bid No. CHPS-2026-07

Prairie View Elementary School Addition and Renovation

Issued: September 21, 2026

Sealed bids due: Thursday, October 22, 2026, 2:00 p.m. Central Time, District Office, 410 Main Street, Cedar Hollow, Minnesota 55319. Bids received after the stated time will be returned unopened.

Architect: Lindgren Bauer Architects, 88 River Street, Mankato, Minnesota. Contact: Anna Lindgren, alindgren@lindgrenbauer.example

**Page 2**

### 1. Project Description

The work consists of a two-story, 22,000 square foot classroom addition and the renovation of 18,000 square feet of the existing 1968 classroom wing at Prairie View Elementary School, 1200 Prairie View Drive, Cedar Hollow, Minnesota.

The school will remain occupied throughout construction. Work inside the existing building is limited to the summer break, June 14 through August 20, 2027, except as approved in writing by the District.

The Owner's estimate of probable construction cost is $18,200,000.

### 2. Delivery Method and Contract

Design-bid-build. A single lump-sum prime contract will be awarded to the lowest responsive and responsible bidder. Contract forms: AIA A101-2017 and AIA A201-2017 as modified by the District's Supplementary Conditions in the Project Manual.

**Page 3**

### 3. Schedule

Mandatory pre-bid meeting and site walk: Thursday, October 8, 2026, 10:00 a.m. Central Time, Prairie View Elementary main office. Bids from firms not represented at the mandatory meeting will be rejected.

Deadline for written questions: Thursday, October 15, 2026, 2:00 p.m. Central Time, by email to the Architect.

Final addendum: no later than Monday, October 19, 2026.

Bid opening: immediately after the bid deadline, District Office.

Anticipated award: School Board meeting, Monday, November 9, 2026.

Notice to Proceed: Tuesday, December 1, 2026.

Substantial Completion: Friday, August 20, 2027.

Final Completion: Friday, September 17, 2027.

**Page 4**

### 4. Bid Security and Bonds

Each bid shall be accompanied by a bid bond or certified check in the amount of five percent (5%) of the base bid.

The successful bidder shall furnish a performance bond and a payment bond, each for one hundred percent (100%) of the contract sum, within ten (10) days after notice of award, from a surety licensed in Minnesota.

### 5. Licensing and Responsible Contractor

Bidders shall submit the Responsible Contractor Verification of Compliance under Minnesota Statutes section 16C.285 with the bid. A bid without the verification will be rejected.

The Contractor shall hold a City of Cedar Hollow Commercial Building Contractor License, Class A, before execution of the contract.

### 6. Wages

Minnesota prevailing wage rates apply to all work. The Contractor and all subcontractors shall submit certified payroll weekly.

**Page 5**

### 7. Insurance

Commercial general liability: $2,000,000 each occurrence and $4,000,000 general aggregate, per project.

Automobile liability: $1,000,000 combined single limit.

Umbrella or excess liability: $10,000,000.

Workers' compensation: statutory limits; employer's liability $1,000,000.

The Contractor shall provide builder's risk insurance for the full replacement value of the work.

### 8. Liquidated Damages

Liquidated damages of $2,500 per calendar day will be assessed for each day the work remains incomplete after the date of Substantial Completion.

**Page 6**

### 9. Existing Conditions

A Hazardous Materials Survey of the 1968 wing (2025) is included in the Project Manual as Attachment C. Abatement of identified materials will be performed by a separate contractor under contract to the District before renovation begins. The Contractor shall coordinate with the abatement contractor and shall stop work and notify the District upon discovery of suspect materials not identified in the survey.

A geotechnical report dated June 2026 is included in the Project Manual for information only.

### 10. Alternates

Alternate 1: Synthetic turf playfield in lieu of seeded lawn (add).

Alternate 2: LED gymnasium lighting replacement (add).

**Page 7**

### 11. Bid Submittals

Each bid shall include: the Bid Form with base bid and both alternates; bid security; the Responsible Contractor Verification of Compliance; acknowledgment of all addenda on the Bid Form; and a signed Non-Collusion Affidavit.

The apparent low bidder shall submit a list of subcontractors performing work valued at five percent (5%) or more of the base bid within twenty-four (24) hours after bid opening.

Bids that are incomplete, conditioned, or that fail to acknowledge all addenda may be rejected as non-responsive.

**Page 8**

### 12. Bidding Documents

Drawings: 186 sheets, dated September 14, 2026. Project Manual: Divisions 00 through 33, including Attachment C (Hazardous Materials Survey) and the geotechnical report. Bidding documents are available from the Architect's online plan room.

The District reserves the right to reject any and all bids and to waive informalities.
