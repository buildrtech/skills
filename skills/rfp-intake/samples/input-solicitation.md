# INVITATION TO BID

**Project:** Ridgeview Elementary School Classroom Addition
**Owner:** Ridgeview Unified School District, 1400 Canyon Road, Ridgeview, CO
**Architect:** Holloway Peterson Architects, Project No. 2519
**ITB No.:** RUSD-2026-014
**Issued:** August 18, 2026

## 1. Project description

The Work consists of a single-story, 14,200 SF classroom addition to the
existing Ridgeview Elementary School, including eight classrooms, a shared
learning commons, restrooms, mechanical and electrical rooms, and connection
to the existing corridor at Grid Line 7. Site work includes a new bus
drop-off loop, storm drainage, and relocation of the existing playground.

The existing school will remain occupied during construction. Work within
the existing building is limited to the corridor connection and must occur
between June 8, 2027 and August 6, 2027.

Engineer's estimate: $6,900,000.

## 2. Delivery and contract

Design-bid-build. The Owner will award a single lump-sum contract to the
lowest responsive, responsible bidder. The contract form is AIA A101-2017
with the District's Supplementary Conditions (Section 00 73 00).

## 4. Bid security and bonds

Each bid shall be accompanied by a bid bond of 5% of the base bid. The
successful bidder shall furnish performance and payment bonds each in the
amount of 100% of the contract sum within ten days of Notice of Award.

## 5. Bidder qualifications

Bidders must hold a current Colorado general contractor registration and a
City of Ridgeview Class A building contractor license at the time of bid.
Bidders must submit AIA A305 Contractor's Qualification Statement with the
bid, including three completed K-12 projects of at least $4,000,000 within
the last five years.

## 6. Wage requirements

This project is funded in part by state Building Excellent Schools Today
(BEST) grant funds. Colorado prevailing wage requirements under C.R.S.
24-92-201 et seq. apply. Certified payroll shall be submitted weekly through
the District's LCPtracker account.

## 7. Liquidated damages

Liquidated damages of $2,500 per calendar day apply after the Substantial
Completion date, and $1,000 per calendar day after Final Completion.

## 8. Insurance

Per Section 00 73 00, Article 11: commercial general liability $2,000,000
per occurrence / $4,000,000 aggregate; auto $1,000,000; umbrella
$5,000,000; workers' compensation statutory. The Contractor shall provide
builder's risk insurance for the full contract value.

## 9. Site conditions

A geotechnical report prepared by Front Range Geotechnical dated March 3,
2026 is included in the Project Manual as Appendix A for information only.
The report identifies expansive clay soils and recommends over-excavation
and replacement to a depth of 4 feet below footings. The Contractor shall
satisfy itself as to subsurface conditions. Supplementary Conditions
paragraph 3.7.4 deletes AIA A201 Section 3.7.4 (Concealed or Unknown
Conditions).
