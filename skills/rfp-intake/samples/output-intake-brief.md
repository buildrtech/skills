## Risk flags

- **Bond close to the single-job limit.** Estimate $18,200,000 against a $20,000,000 single-job limit (91%). A bid over the estimate, or growth through change orders, could exceed what the surety will write. Confirm with the surety before bid day. (ITB p. 2, §1)
- **No float on the renovation.** Work in the existing building can only happen from June 14 to August 20, 2027, the same day as Substantial Completion, and liquidated damages are $2,500 a calendar day. Any slip in the summer renovation goes straight to liquidated damages, about $75,000 a month, with no room to recover. Offsetting: The addition can start at Notice to Proceed in December 2026, so only the renovation is tied to the window. (ITB p. 2, §1)
- **Hazardous materials survey not in the package.** Attachment C is referenced but sits in the Project Manual, which wasn't supplied. The District's own abatement contractor works before the renovation, and we must stop work if unidentified materials turn up. Without the survey there is no way to judge how much abatement could eat into the 10-week summer window. Offsetting: Abatement is the District's contract, not ours. (ITB p. 6, §9)
- **Contract changes not reviewed.** The AIA documents are modified by Supplementary Conditions in the Project Manual. Risk allocation (delay damages, concealed conditions, indemnity) can't be confirmed until the Supplementary Conditions are read. (ITB p. 2, §2)
- **Builder's risk on the contractor.** The contractor insures the full replacement value of the work. Add the premium to the estimate and confirm the broker will cover renovation inside an occupied 1968 building. (ITB p. 5, §7)
