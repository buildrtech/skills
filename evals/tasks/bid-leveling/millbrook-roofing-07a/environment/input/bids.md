# Bid package 07A: low-slope roofing, tapered insulation, and sheet metal

**Project:** Millbrook Avenue Parking Garage, City of Millbrook — low-slope
roof over the enclosed ground-floor transit concourse and the stair and
elevator towers (synthetic project)
**Package:** 07A, Sections 07 26 00 Vapor Retarders, 07 22 16 Roof Board
Insulation, 07 54 23 TPO Membrane Roofing, 07 62 00 Sheet Metal Flashing and
Trim, 07 72 33 Roof Hatches
**Bids due:** August 25, 2026. Addendum 1 (August 14) revised the tapered
layout and cricket locations on A-401 and solicited Alternate 1 (80-mil TPO
membrane in lieu of 60-mil).

Three bids came in. They are transcribed below as received, one section per
document, with the file name each one arrived as.

---

## 07A scope sheet issued with the bid package

Every 07A bidder was asked to carry all of the following:

1. Self-adhered vapor retarder over metal deck (07 26 00)
2. Tapered polyiso insulation, R-30 average, crickets to drains (07 22 16)
3. 1/2" gypsum cover board
4. 60-mil TPO membrane, fully adhered (07 54 23)
5. Sheet metal coping, edge metal, and counterflashing (07 62 00)
6. Flashing of mechanical curbs and pipe penetrations (curbs furnished by 15A)
7. Walkway pads at rooftop units and roof hatch
8. Hoisting of roofing materials to the roof
9. Perimeter warning line and fall protection for own work
10. 20-year manufacturer NDL system warranty
11. Roof hatch, furnish and install (07 72 33)

Roof drains and overflow drains are in the plumbing package 22A. Metal wall
panels at the mechanical screen (07 42 13) are in the metal panel package
07B and are not part of 07A. Mechanical curbs are furnished by 15A and
flashed by 07A.
