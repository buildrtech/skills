## Plug summary in plain language

Four plugs and one adjustment moved the totals. Every one of them is the
estimator's decision from `estimator-decisions.json`; none was chosen here.

- **Ironwood, roof hatch, $6,850.** Ironwood's proposal says "Roof hatch by
  others" (IR-4). D. Okafor (8/27) carried Cardinal's itemized roof hatch
  line, bid form row 8, as the plug. That is a leveling convenience, not a
  statement that Ironwood would charge $6,850.
- **Blue Heron, vapor retarder, $38,600.** The email says "Vapor retarder by
  others" (BH-2). D. Okafor (8/27) used the self-adhered vapor retarder line
  from the 7/28 GC estimate, line 07 26 00.
- **Blue Heron, walkway pads, $4,200.** The email never mentions walkway pads
  in either direction (BH-4), so the scope sheet item is a gap, not an
  inclusion. D. Okafor (8/27) carried Cardinal's itemized walkway pad line,
  bid form row 7.
- **Cardinal, hoisting, $9,500.** Bid form row 11 reads "By GC" with no
  amount (CC-3). D. Okafor (8/27) used the Lakeshore Crane quote dated 8/12
  for two crane days at the garage, GC estimate line 01 54 00.
- **Cardinal, adjustment, -$27,400.** Cardinal priced metal wall panels at
  the mechanical screen (07 42 13) inside its total at $27,400, bid form row
  12 (CC-2). That work is in the metal panel package 07B, so it comes out of
  the 07A comparison as a sourced adjustment rather than by editing the bid.

One gap is still unresolved: Blue Heron's warranty. The email offers a
"Manufacturer's standard 20-year membrane warranty" (BH-3), which is not the
20-year NDL system warranty Section 07 54 23 calls for. Until Blue Heron
answers, its $614,700 is incomplete and is not a low number.

## What could move the ranking

- **The unresolved warranty gap.** If Blue Heron's warranty wording turns out
  to price the NDL system warranty, its leveled total of $614,700 becomes
  complete and it lands above Cardinal's $580,850 and below Ironwood's
  $619,250. If it does not, Blue Heron has to add the NDL warranty and its
  number goes up.
- **Cardinal's bid form does not foot.** Rows 1 through 13 sum to $600,550
  against a stated total of $598,750, a difference of $1,800. Which number
  governs changes Cardinal's leveled total by that amount.
- **The bond is on three different bases.** Cardinal's total already carries
  a $8,900 performance and payment bond; Ironwood excludes the bond and would
  add 1.5% if required; Blue Heron simply says "Bond not included." Nothing
  in the leveled totals reflects a bond decision. Once the common basis is
  set, each bid that is off it needs a sourced adjustment.
- **The voluntary alternate.** Cardinal's -$31,000 deduct for a mechanically
  attached membrane in lieu of fully adhered is not the specified system. It
  is not in any leveled total and needs a review decision before it counts.
- **Alternate 1 pricing.** Blue Heron $18,750, Ironwood $21,300, Cardinal
  $24,100 for the 80-mil membrane. All three priced it, so it does not change
  who is complete, but it changes the spread if the owner takes it.
