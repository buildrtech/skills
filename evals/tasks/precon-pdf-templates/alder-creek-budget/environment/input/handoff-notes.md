# Handoff notes for the Alder Creek budget export

From: Dana Whitfield, Preconstruction Coordinator, Pinewood Construction Group
Date: 2026-09-02

`alder-creek-budget-export.csv` is the budget export from our estimating
system for the Alder Creek Elementary School Classroom Addition, estimate
dated 2026-09-01 at the 90% construction documents stage. It is going to the
Alder Creek Unified School District board facilities committee on Thursday.

How the export reads:

- "Direct cost" rows are the line items, grouped under their CSI division.
  Each division ends with a "Division NN subtotal" row that our system
  prints from the estimate, not from the rows above it.
- "Markup" rows sit on top of the direct cost. The estimating contingency
  is carried at roughly 5% of direct cost and rounded.
- "Total" rows are the direct cost subtotal, the markups, and the grand
  total the district will see.
- "Alternate" rows are priced separately and are not in the grand total.
  Alternate 2 is a deduct.

Notes to print on the document, in this order:

1. Pricing is based on the 90% construction documents dated 2026-08-20.
2. Excludes furniture, technology equipment, and district-furnished
   playground equipment. Alternates are priced separately and are not
   included in the total.
3. Estimating contingency is carried at approximately 5% of direct cost.

Prepared by Pinewood Construction Group for Alder Creek Unified School
District.
