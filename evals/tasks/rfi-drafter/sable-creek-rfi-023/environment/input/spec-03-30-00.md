# Excerpt: Specification Section 03 30 00, Cast-in-Place Concrete

Only the paragraphs below were pulled. Paragraph numbers are as printed in
the project manual.

## Part 1, General

- 1.4.B: "Design Mixtures: Submit for each concrete mixture. Submit
  alternate design mixtures when characteristics of materials, Project
  conditions, weather, test results, or other circumstances warrant
  adjustments. Design mixtures are subject to review by the Structural
  Engineer before placement."

## Part 2, Products

- 2.6.A: "Footings and Grade Beams: Compressive strength 3,000 psi at 28
  days; maximum water-cementitious materials ratio 0.50; slump 4 inches,
  plus or minus 1 inch."
- 2.6.C: "Slabs-on-Grade: Compressive strength 4,000 psi at 28 days;
  maximum water-cementitious materials ratio 0.45; slump 4 inches, plus or
  minus 1 inch; air content 3 percent maximum for interior slabs to receive
  a hard-troweled finish."
- 2.6.D: "Foundation Walls and Piers: Compressive strength 4,000 psi at 28
  days; maximum water-cementitious materials ratio 0.45; air content 5.5
  percent, plus or minus 1.5 percent."
