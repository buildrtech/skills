# INVITATION FOR BIDS

*Everything in this package is synthetic. Every name, date, amount, and
statute reference is invented for a review exercise.*

**Project:** Fire Station No. 4 Replacement
**Owner:** City of Brannock Falls, Washington, 210 Front Street, Brannock Falls, WA
**Architect:** Sorensen Vail Architecture PLLC, Project No. 24-117
**IFB No.:** BF-26-031
**Issued:** September 8, 2026

## 1. Project description

The Work consists of a new two-story, 16,400 SF fire station on the
existing Station 4 parcel at 880 Kestrel Ridge Road, including four
drive-through apparatus bays, dormitories for twelve, a decontamination
suite, a fitness room, a day room and kitchen, an emergency operations room,
and a standby generator with a 72-hour fuel supply. Site work includes a new
apparatus apron, a signalized egress onto Kestrel Ridge Road, stormwater
vaults, and frontage improvements.

The existing Station 4, built in 1968, occupies the north third of the
parcel and shall remain fully in service, with unobstructed apparatus
egress at all times, until the new station is accepted for occupancy. The
existing building is then to be demolished under this Contract.

The building is designated a Risk Category IV essential facility under the
Washington State Building Code.

Engineer's estimate for the Base Bid: $11,400,000.

## 2. Delivery and contract

Design-bid-build. The City will award a single lump-sum contract to the
lowest responsive, responsible bidder based on the Base Bid plus any
Additive Alternates the City elects to accept, in numerical order. The
contract form is the City of Brannock Falls Public Works Contract (Section
00 52 00) with the City's General Conditions (Section 00 72 00) and
Supplementary Conditions (Section 00 73 00).

The Contractor shall warrant all Work for two years from the date of
Substantial Completion, in place of the one-year correction period in the
General Conditions (Supplementary Conditions paragraph 12.2.2).

## 4. Bid security, bonds, and retainage

Each bid shall be accompanied by a bid bond of 5% of the total bid,
including all Additive Alternates. The successful bidder shall furnish a
performance bond and a payment bond, each in the amount of 100% of the
contract sum, within ten days of Notice of Award.

The City will withhold retainage of 5% of each progress payment under RCW
60.28. Retainage will be released 60 days after final acceptance, subject to
clearances from the Department of Revenue, Employment Security, and Labor
and Industries.

## 5. Bidder qualifications

Bidders must hold a current Washington State contractor registration under
RCW 18.27 at the time of bid and must not be on the Labor and Industries
debarment list.

Bidders must submit a Bidder's Qualification Statement (Section 00 45 13)
with the bid demonstrating:

1. Two completed essential-facility projects (fire station, police station,
   emergency operations center, or hospital), each with a construction value
   of at least $8,000,000 and each designed to Risk Category IV, completed
   within the last seven years.
2. A proposed superintendent who served as superintendent on at least one
   of the projects listed under item 1.
3. Successful completion of at least one project under Washington
   prevailing wage requirements within the last five years.

Bids that do not meet the qualifications in this Section will be rejected
as non-responsive.

## 6. Wage and labor requirements

This is a public work. Washington State prevailing wage requirements under
RCW 39.12 apply, using the Labor and Industries rates in effect on the bid
due date. The Contractor and every subcontractor shall file a Statement of
Intent to Pay Prevailing Wages before the first payment and an Affidavit of
Wages Paid before final payment, and shall submit certified payroll weekly
through the Labor and Industries online portal.

Under RCW 39.04.320 and Brannock Falls Municipal Code 3.60, apprentices
shall perform no less than 15% of the total labor hours on this Contract.
The City may assess a penalty of $1,000 for each percentage point below the
requirement.

## 7. Liquidated damages and delay

Liquidated damages of $3,200 per calendar day apply for each day the Work
is not substantially complete after the Substantial Completion date, and
$750 per calendar day for each day after the Final Completion date.

Supplementary Conditions paragraph 8.3.3 provides that the Contractor's sole
remedy for any delay caused by the City, the Architect, or a separate
contractor is an extension of the Contract Time. No damages for delay,
disruption, or acceleration will be paid.

## 8. Insurance

Per Section 00 73 00, Article 11, the Contractor shall maintain:

- Commercial general liability: $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000
  general aggregate, per-project aggregate endorsement.
- Automobile liability: $2,000,000 combined single limit.
- Umbrella or excess liability: $10,000,000.
- Workers' compensation: statutory, with Washington stop-gap employer's
  liability of $1,000,000.
- Contractor's pollution liability: $2,000,000 per claim, covering the
  abatement and demolition Work described in Section 9.

The City will carry builder's risk insurance for the full contract value.
The City, the Architect, and their consultants shall be named as additional
insureds on all liability policies on a primary and non-contributory basis.

## 9. Site conditions and hazardous materials

A geotechnical report prepared by Pacific Cascade Geosciences dated April
14, 2026 is included in the Project Manual as Appendix B and is a Contract
Document. The report identifies groundwater at 6 feet below existing grade
and loose silty fill to a depth of 9 feet across the south half of the
parcel, and recommends ground improvement with aggregate piers under all
building foundations and the apparatus apron. General Conditions paragraph
4.7 (Differing Site Conditions) is retained and unmodified.

A hazardous materials survey of the existing Station 4 prepared by Rainier
Environmental dated May 6, 2026 is included as Appendix C. The survey
identifies asbestos-containing floor tile and mastic, transite wall panels
at the apparatus bays, and lead-based paint on the hose tower. Abatement of
all identified materials, and demolition of the existing building after the
new station is accepted, are included in the Contractor's scope. The
Contractor shall employ a certified asbestos abatement contractor and shall
file all notifications required by the Puget Sound Clean Air Agency.
