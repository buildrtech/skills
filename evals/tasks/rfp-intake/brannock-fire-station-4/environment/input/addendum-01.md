This Addendum forms part of the Contract Documents and modifies the
Invitation for Bids dated September 8, 2026 as noted below. Acknowledge
receipt of this Addendum on the Bid Form.

## A1-1. Bid due date

Section 3, Schedule of events: the bid due date is changed from October 13,
2026 to **October 15, 2026, 2:00 PM**, City Clerk's Office, 210 Front
Street. All other dates in Section 3 are unchanged. The pre-bid conference
on September 22, 2026 remains mandatory.

## A1-2. Additive Alternate No. 2

Section 11, item 1: add Additive Alternate No. 2, a 90 kW rooftop
photovoltaic array with battery storage, per the revised Bid Form (Section
00 41 13) attached to this Addendum. Bidders shall enter a price for both
Additive Alternates; a blank alternate will be treated as non-responsive.

## A1-3. Questions received to date

**Q1.** Will the City accept a retainage bond in place of retainage
withheld from progress payments?

**A1.** Yes. Under RCW 60.28.011 the Contractor may submit a retainage bond
on the City's form (Section 00 61 16) in place of the 5% retainage
described in Section 4 of the Invitation for Bids.

**Q2.** Does the two-year warranty in Section 2 apply to the standby
generator and the photovoltaic array?

**A2.** Yes. Manufacturer warranties on equipment run in addition to, not
in place of, the Contractor's two-year warranty.
