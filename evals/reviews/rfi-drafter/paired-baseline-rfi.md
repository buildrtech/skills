## Question

Please confirm whether Area C at grids 2-4/B-D should receive a revised slab mix meeting 03 30 00 paragraph 2.4.B, with its air requirement established for the actual slab use and finish, in place of returned Mix C, or provide the structural engineer’s written direction for an alternative? S-110 General Notes 2 and 8 establish a 3,800 psi minimum but make the more stringent specification govern; paragraph 2.4.B requires 4,500 psi minimum at 28 days and maximum w/cm 0.44. Returned submittal 03 30 00-07 lists Mix C at 3,800 psi and w/cm 0.47, and its “No Exceptions Taken” action expressly does not waive contract compliance. The supplied excerpts do not establish Area C’s finish or interior/exterior designation, so applicability of the specification’s conditional air limit remains unverified.

## References

- **S-110, General Note 2:** “the more stringent requirement governs unless the structural engineer directs otherwise in writing.”
- **S-110, General Note 8:** Slabs on grade require 3,800 psi minimum at 28 days. The supplied S-110 excerpt locates Area C at grids 2-4/B-D and gives no finish or interior/exterior designation.
- **03 30 00, paragraph 2.4.B:** Slabs on grade require 4,500 psi minimum at 28 days and maximum water-cementitious ratio 0.44. Air content is limited to 2.5 percent for interior slabs receiving a hard-troweled finish.
- **Returned submittal 03 30 00-07:** Engineer action: “No Exceptions Taken.” “Review does not waive compliance with contract documents.” Mix C: slab, 3,800 psi, w/cm 0.47, air 2 percent. Mix W: walls, 4,500 psi, w/cm 0.44, air 5 percent.

## Suggested resolution

For design-team confirmation, submit a revised Area C slab mix meeting 4,500 psi minimum at 28 days and maximum w/cm 0.44, with air content appropriate to the confirmed use and finish. If Area C is an interior hard-troweled slab, the specified maximum air content is 2.5 percent. Mix C’s 2 percent air meets that conditional ceiling, but its strength and w/cm do not meet paragraph 2.4.B.

Mix W is not proposed as a substitute: it is identified for walls, and its 5 percent air exceeds the conditional 2.5 percent limit if that limit applies to Area C. Matching strength and w/cm alone does not establish suitability for this slab. Area C’s use and finish must be established to finalize the air requirement. The returned submittal does not establish a written exception to the governing requirements.
