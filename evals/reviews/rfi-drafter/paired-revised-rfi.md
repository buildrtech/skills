## Question

Please confirm the proposed compliant slab-mix basis below for Area C, including whether the interior hard-troweled slab air limit applies, or provide the structural engineer’s written alternate direction? S-110 General Notes 2 and 8 and specification 03 30 00 paragraph 2.4.B establish a governing minimum strength of 4,500 psi at 28 days and maximum w/cm of 0.44. Returned submittal 03 30 00-07 lists Mix C at 3,800 psi and w/cm 0.47, outside those requirements, and states that review does not waive contract compliance despite the “No Exceptions Taken” action. The S-110 excerpt does not identify Area C’s finish or interior/exterior designation, so applicability of the specification’s conditional air limit remains unconfirmed.

## References

- **S-110, General Note 2:** “the more stringent requirement governs unless the structural engineer directs otherwise in writing.”
- **S-110, General Note 8:** Slabs on grade require 3,800 psi minimum at 28 days.
- **S-110, Area C excerpt:** Slab at grids 2-4/B-D; no finish or interior/exterior designation supplied.
- **Specification 03 30 00, paragraph 2.4.B:** Slabs on grade require 4,500 psi minimum at 28 days and maximum water-cementitious ratio of 0.44; air content is limited to 2.5 percent maximum for interior slabs receiving a hard-troweled finish.
- **Returned submittal 03 30 00-07:** Engineer action “No Exceptions Taken”; review does not waive compliance with contract documents. Mix C: slab, 3,800 psi, w/cm 0.47, air 2 percent. Mix W: walls, 4,500 psi, w/cm 0.44, air 5 percent.

## Suggested resolution

For design-team confirmation, propose revising and resubmitting the slab mix to provide at least 4,500 psi at 28 days and w/cm no greater than 0.44. If Area C is an interior slab receiving a hard-troweled finish, the revised mix must also provide air content no greater than 2.5 percent. Confirm Area C’s designation and finish before establishing the applicable air requirement; the supplied excerpts do not establish an air criterion for other conditions.

Mix C’s 2 percent air meets the conditional 2.5 percent maximum if that condition applies, but its strength and w/cm do not comply. Mix W meets the stated strength and w/cm limits but is designated for walls, and its 5 percent air exceeds the limit if Area C is an interior hard-troweled slab. It is not proposed as an unconditional slab substitution. The returned submittal does not authorize a deviation from the specification.
