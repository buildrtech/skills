## Basis

- Bidders and amounts come only from the extraction files listed in the Summary table; the script adds nothing.
- Each bidder's base bid stays at the submission level. Itemized amounts, where a bid gives them, are shown but never spread across rows.
- Plugs are estimator decisions recorded in the decisions file. They level a bidder's gap for comparison and are not the bidder's price.
- This is arithmetic on extracted evidence, not an award recommendation and not a scope review.
