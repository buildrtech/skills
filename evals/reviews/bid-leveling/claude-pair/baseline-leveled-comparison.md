## Questions to send each bidder

Drawn from the unresolved gap and the review items above. Answers come from the
bidders and the GC, not from this comparison.

**Vale Fence Ltd — welded option**

1. Your bid states "Cleanup excluded." Package F, addendum C, page 2 requires
   cleanup. Please price cleanup for this option, or confirm in writing that you
   will not carry it. (The $1,800 in the matrix is our own carry from
   self-perform estimate F-12, not your number.)
2. Your "Alt 1: motor upgrade on main gate" is being compared as solicited
   Alternate M. Please confirm the numbering refers to the same alternate.
3. You submitted a welded and a bolted option on the same date. Both
   installation methods are permitted; please confirm both prices stand and tell
   us which one you would hold if asked for one.

**Vale Fence Ltd — bolted option**

1. Your bid says "Warranty subject to final terms." Package F, addendum C,
   page 2 requires a two-year installation warranty. Please confirm the two-year
   installation warranty is included in the $44,000 base at no added cost, or
   state the added cost. This is the one unresolved gap holding this option's
   leveled total incomplete; no plug was approved for it.
2. Your "Option C: motor upgrade on main gate" is being compared as solicited
   Alternate M. Please confirm the labels refer to the same alternate.
3. Same question as the welded option about which of your two options governs.

**Quarry Fence Co.**

1. Your "Alt 1: extra pedestrian gate, ADD $3,400" was not solicited; only the
   gate motor upgrade (Alternate M) was. Please confirm the $3,400 is outside
   your $47,100 base and that no part of the pedestrian gate is priced in the
   base.
2. Your "Alt 2: motor upgrade on main gate" is being compared as solicited
   Alternate M. Please confirm the numbering refers to the same alternate.

**For the GC / estimator**

1. Confirm whether the pedestrian gate should be solicited from all three
   bidders, since only Quarry priced it.
2. No plug was approved for the bolted option's warranty; that option stays
   incomplete until Vale answers.

## Basis

- Bidders and amounts come only from the extraction files listed in the Summary table; the script adds nothing.
- Each bidder's base bid stays at the submission level. Itemized amounts, where a bid gives them, are shown but never spread across rows.
- Plugs are estimator decisions recorded in the decisions file. They level a bidder's gap for comparison and are not the bidder's price.
- This is arithmetic on extracted evidence, not an award recommendation and not a scope review.
