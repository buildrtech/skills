# Synthetic ITB HC-27-09 — Harbor Crossing Pump House

## 1. Work and award
The Town of Harbor Crossing seeks one lump-sum design-bid-build contract for
replacement of pumps, controls, and a standby generator. Maintain the existing
pump house in operation throughout the work. Drawings P1–P8 and the hazardous
materials survey are referenced but are not included in this test package.
The town carries builder's risk. No owner budget is stated.

## 2. Dates and delivery
Mandatory site visit: March 4, 2027, 09:00 local town time.
Questions: March 8, 2027, 12:00 local town time.
Bids: March 12, 2027, 14:00 local town time, sealed envelope at Town Hall.
Award and construction start dates are not stated.

## 3. Responsiveness
Include signed Bid Form, 10% bid bond calculated on base plus all alternates,
contractor registration, and acknowledgment of each addendum. Provide a
subcontractor list within two hours after bids close. Omission of the list
makes the bid non-responsive. Bid Form requires Alternate A, a generator
acoustic enclosure. A blank alternate price makes the bid non-responsive.
Successful bidder provides 100% performance and payment bonds within seven
days of award. CGL: $1 million per occurrence / $2 million aggregate;
auto: $1 million; workers' compensation: statutory.

## 4. Qualification and contract
Bidder must document three completed operating-water-facility projects in the
last four years. Weekly certified payroll is required. No wage-rate schedule
is included. Contractor carries all identified hazardous-material abatement.
The survey quantities are not reproduced here. Delay compensation is limited
to time extensions. Warranty: one year after acceptance.
